The questions

Asked at the gate

The same answers the gate pages carry, so nothing about the house is a mystery before the door.

Is The Signal truly free?

Yes — free, forever. No card, no renewal. Every realm drops in The Signal first; the membership is optional and only for those who want the making-of, the lore and the live session.

What is inside The Watcher?

One 28-day cycle, delivered in four weekly chapters: Week I · The Realm (the month's new being), Week II · The Process (how the vision was made), Week III · The Records (the lore), Week IV · The Session (live build, Q&A, the vote).

Why $28 a moon?

One full cycle of the realms: 28 days, four chapters, the room and the live session. Take the whole year for $280 and the seat never darkens.

What if it is not for me?

The Watcher carries the seven-night guarantee: write within seven nights of your first payment and the full amount returns. Keep what you received. The Signal stays free either way.

How do I cancel?

Open your membership in your Whop library and cancel there, or write to numinous.support@gmail.com. Your access stays to the end of the cycle you have already paid for. Nothing is paused — you simply stop when you choose.

Do the realms work on any screen?

Yes. Every realm and every session is rendered for phone, tablet and desktop.

Can I use the visions commercially?

The visions are yours to use, including commercially. The files themselves — the Wallpaper Vault download and the written records — may not be resold or redistributed. The license of the Works has the whole of it.

Anything else on your mind — write to numinous.support@gmail.com. A person answers.
